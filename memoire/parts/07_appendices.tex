\chapter{Core Sources of the Literature Review}\label{app:sources}

The thesis cites 86 references, and \cref{ch:review} draws on 35 of them (\cref{sec:lr-intro}). \Cref{tab:sources} summarises the nineteen core sources among these 35, the studies and the report analysed in detail, in the order of the themes of the review: design and data, main findings, limits, and what this thesis takes from each. \multicolumn{5}{@{}l}{\textit{Mobile health and clinical evidence}} \\

\textcite{mao2020} & meta-analysis of 51 randomised trials, 13\,054 participants & SBP $-2.99$~mmHg, HbA1c $-0.39$; $-6.17$~mmHg with professional management; no SBP effect in developing countries & moderate to high heterogeneity; one trial in sub-Saharan Africa & effect conditional on a clinician (\cref{sec:efficacy}) \\
\textcite{zhou2024} & meta-analysis of 20 randomised trials, 16 pooled, 4\,168 participants & SBP $-5.78$~mmHg; larger with eleven or more techniques and several risk factors & low certainty; 15 of 20 trials at high risk of bias & behaviour change techniques \\

\textcite{mihevc2025} & multicentre open-label trial, 128 patients aged 65 or more with both conditions, Slovenia & SBP $-6.9$~mmHg and HbA1c $-0.5$ at twelve months; 10.9\% withdrew in the first month & high-income setting; older adults; not blinded & three-level home protocol adopted (\cref{sec:triage}) \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{5}{@{}l}{\textit{Sensors and IoT}} \\

\textcite{tai2024} & deep learning technique in a three-phase architecture & accurate and interpretable predictions in their evaluation & controlled conditions; connectivity not addressed & layered architecture \\
\textcite{efendi2025} & IoT system (ESP32, MQTT, Firebase), six volunteers & mean absolute percentage error 0.90\%; XGBoost accuracy 0.973 & label a function of the inputs; no blood pressure or glucose; data stored abroad & offline-first storage; example of leakage (\cref{sec:lr-ml}) \\
\textcite{liu2025review} & survey of IoT sensing and machine learning & device categories; seven-layer platform; accuracies of 95--100\%; defects of electronic records & no protocol or flow diagram & record quality; layers \\

\addlinespace
\multicolumn{5}{@{}l}{\textit{Privacy and ethics}} \\
\textcite{alhammad2024} & systematic review of 33 studies of patient perspectives & data concerns hinder adoption; encryption, authentication and transparency build trust & mostly high-income countries; self-reported perceptions & data protection as a design constraint (\cref{sec:lr-privacy}) \\

\normalfont\textbf{Column} & \textbf{Content} & \textbf{Use} \\
\midrule

height\_cm, weight\_kg & empty in every row & not used \\
